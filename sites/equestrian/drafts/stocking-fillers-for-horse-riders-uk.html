The short answer: the best stocking fillers for horse riders are small things that get used up or worn out on the yard – horse treats and licks, plaiting bands, a new brush, long riding socks, a spare pair of gloves, a hi-vis hatband – plus one or two fun extras. Most of the ideas below were around £2–£20 at Naylors or GS Equestrian in November 2026. If in doubt, a gift card is the safest choice: Naylors' gift cards start at £5.

Here are ideas grouped by type, so you can mix a few cheap consumables with one slightly nicer thing.

A simple formula for a rider's stocking: one thing for the horse (treats or a lick refill), one consumable for the tack room (plaiting bands, saddle soap or leather balsam), one thing to wear (socks, gloves or a hatband) and one fun extra. That keeps the total roughly in the £20–£40 range at the prices below, and avoids the two most common gift mistakes with riders: buying sized kit in the wrong size, and buying a brand of something they already have strong opinions about. Hats, body protectors and boots need proper fitting, so leave those out of the stocking altogether – a gift card covers them better.
