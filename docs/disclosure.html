Affiliate disclosure

Ad. The Rented Nest is free to read. We pay for it through affiliate links: when you click a link to a retailer and buy something, the retailer may pay us a small commission. The price you pay is the same.

Pages that contain affiliate links are labelled "Ad" at the top, and our Pinterest pins that link to them are labelled too.

Programmes we take part in

  • Amazon Associates (Amazon.co.uk). As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases.
  • Awin. We're an Awin publisher. Links to these retailers go through Awin, which tracks the referral: Beldray, Dimplex, Homebase, La Redoute, Meaco.
  • Sovrn Commerce. Some links to other shops, such as Argos, go through Sovrn Commerce, an affiliate network. If you buy after clicking one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

We'll add each new retailer to this list as it approves us.

What commissions don't change

  • Which products we feature, or what we say about them.
  • The price you pay.
  • Our rule that we only recommend products available from UK retailers.

Prices and availability change often, so check the retailer's page before you buy.
