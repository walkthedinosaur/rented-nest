How to make £10 go further

  • Set the rules first. Agree whether £10 includes postage and wrapping. It saves an awkward "mine cost more" moment.
  • Watch delivery costs. A £7 item with £4 delivery blows the budget. Group it with something you're already ordering, or pick it up in town.
  • Leave time. Personalised and made-to-order gifts take longer in December. Order a couple of weeks before the swap.
  • Use the wishlist. One "would love" and one "please don't" from each person makes a cheap gift feel thoughtful.
  • Wrap simply. Brown paper and string looks deliberate and costs very little – useful when the gift is small.
