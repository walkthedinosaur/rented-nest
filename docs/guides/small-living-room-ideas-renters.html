Magnolia walls and a landlord's sofa don't have to be the end of the story. These changes all go with you when you move.

Light

Layer lamps instead of relying on the ceiling light

A floor lamp in one corner and a table lamp in another makes a small room feel wider and cosier. Warm-white bulbs suit evenings.

Peel-off options – with care

Removable wallpaper and decals exist, but how cleanly they come off depends on the paint underneath. Ask your landlord, and test a small patch.

Layout

Pull furniture off the walls

Even a small gap behind the sofa can make a room feel more considered than pushing everything to the edges.

Choose furniture with legs

Seeing floor underneath a sofa or sideboard makes a room read as bigger.

Use a bookcase as a room divider

In a studio or open-plan flat, an open cube unit separates living and sleeping space while letting light through.
