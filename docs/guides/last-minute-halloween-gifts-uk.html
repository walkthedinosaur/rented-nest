Last-minute scary Halloween gifts (UK) – fun ideas for housemates and friends

Last-minute Halloween gift ideas that are actually fun – skeleton fairy lights, prank spiders, eyeball sweets, horror party games and skull mugs – plus how to get them in time.

Short answer: for a last-minute Halloween gift, go small, silly and spooky: skeleton or pumpkin fairy lights, a realistic fake spider to hide somewhere, a bag of eyeball sweets, a horror party game, or a skull mug. If you're ordering online, check the delivery date shown at checkout before you pay – if it's after 31 October, pick something up in town instead.

These ideas suit a shared house or a small flat: nothing that needs screwing to a wall, nothing that leaves marks, and most of it can come out again next year.

Spooky bits for their room

Decorations make an easy gift because the whole house gets to enjoy them. Battery-powered ones are the renter's friend – no trailing cables and nothing stuck to the paintwork.

Skeleton or pumpkin fairy lights

A string of battery fairy lights with little skulls, skeletons or pumpkins on it is daft, cheap and instantly turns a bedroom into a haunted house. Drape it over a shelf or a mirror rather than sticking it up.

Getting it in time

  • Check the delivery date. The date shown at checkout is what counts – don't assume next day.
  • Buy local if it's too late. Many supermarkets and high-street shops carry Halloween ranges in late October.
  • Battery, not flame. Battery lights and tea lights are safer in a shared house and won't scorch anything.
  • Leave no trace. Avoid sticky tape, glue dots and fake cobweb on painted walls – it can pull paint off and cost you at check-out.
