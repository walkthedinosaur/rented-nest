What we collect

This site has no accounts or comments, and sets no advertising cookies. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits in aggregate – it does not use cookies or local storage and does not track you across other sites.

Email sign-up. Some guides offer a free checklist by email. If you sign up, we collect your email address (and first name, if you give it) so we can send you the checklist and occasional emails with new guides and seasonal tips. We only add you after you click the confirmation link we send (double opt-in), and every email has an unsubscribe link – use it at any time and we'll stop emailing you. Our lawful basis is your consent. We don't sell or share your email address with retailers or advertisers.

Third parties

  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as IP addresses to keep the service secure.
  • Analytics. Cloudflare Web Analytics receives basic technical data (such as the page visited, referring site, browser and device type) to produce aggregate visit statistics. It is cookie-free.
  • Email. Our email list is run by Kit (ConvertKit), which stores sign-up details and sends our emails on our behalf, and may record whether emails are opened or links clicked. The sign-up form loads from Kit's servers. See Kit's own privacy policy for how it handles data.
  • Fonts. Our typefaces load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.
  • Retailers. When you click an affiliate link, the retailer or affiliate network may set cookies to track the referral. Their own privacy policies apply once you're on their site.
