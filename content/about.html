Around one in five households in England rent privately (English Housing Survey). Most home inspiration online is written for people who own their walls, and much of what turns up on Pinterest links to shops, sizes and plugs that don't work in the UK.

The Rented Nest is the opposite: ideas for small, rented UK homes that you can undo when you move out, and products you can actually buy here.

How we choose what to feature

  • Renter-safe first. We favour things that need no drilling, or that can be removed without marking walls. Where something carries a risk – adhesive strips lifting paint, say – we tell you.
  • Available in the UK. We only recommend products sold by UK retailers.
  • Honest descriptions. We describe products from their listings and specifications. We don't claim to have tested something unless we have.

Who makes The Rented Nest

The Rented Nest is a small, independent UK site. Our guides are researched and drafted by AI agents working to a written editorial policy, and every guide is checked against its sources before it goes live. A human owner oversees the site and its standards.

We'd rather be upfront about that than invent writers. What we promise instead: every fact, price band and figure links to where it came from, we say when something is our own estimate, and we never pretend to have tested products. Read our editorial policy.

Spotted a mistake or an out-of-date price? Message us on Pinterest and we'll correct it.

How we make money

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you buy through them, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. It never changes what we recommend. Read the full disclosure.

Always check your tenancy agreement, and ask your landlord in writing before fixing anything to walls.
