Updated October 2026

Black Friday 2026 falls on Friday 27 November, with Cyber Monday on Monday 30 November. In practice, many retailers now start their offers well before the day itself, and some run them across most of November.

If you rent, the sales are a good time to buy a few things you'd want anyway – but only a few. The best buys are things that come with you when you move, things that make a cold or damp flat easier to live in through winter, and things that make a small space work harder. Anything you'd have to screw to the wall, or leave behind at check-out, is rarely a bargain.

This page is a shortlist of categories worth watching, with typical prices from our in-depth guides so you can tell whether a "deal" really is one. We'll add live deals from about 20 November. Prices change fast during the sales – sometimes several times a day – so always check the live price before you buy.

How to spot a real Black Friday deal

Check the price history. For Amazon, CamelCamelCamel is a free price tracker that shows how an item's price has moved over time, and can email you when it drops below a price you set (alerts need a free account). Which? also suggests PriceRunner for comparing prices across shops.

Note the price in early November. Write down the price of anything you're considering now, or add it to a wish list. If the "Black Friday price" is the same as it was a few weeks earlier, it isn't a deal.

Don't panic-buy. When Which? tracked 175 Black Friday 2024 deals across eight retailers, every one of them was the same price or cheaper at some point in the six months before or after – and none was at its cheapest price of the year on Black Friday itself. Its advice: there's no need to rush, and if you weren't planning to buy it, it isn't a deal.

Check who's selling it. On marketplaces, look at who sells and ships the item. Which? warns about fake and counterfeit products – which matters most for anything that plugs in and heats up.

Check the returns window. Citizens Advice explains that you can usually cancel goods bought online during a 14-day "cooling off" period. Retailers' own returns policies vary, so check before you order – especially for bulky items that are awkward to send back.
