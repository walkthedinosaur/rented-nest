Buying for someone who rents? Skip the clutter and go clever: a lamp with no cable, a projector that turns a magnolia wall into a cinema, a splashback that peels off again. Everything here is compact, moves house easily and needs no drill. Order early – retailers warn that delivery slows in December.

Screwdrivers, an Allen key set and a tape measure cover most flat-pack and moving-day jobs – and a slim case fits under the sink, because there's no shed in a flat.
