Updated October 2026

Buying for someone who rents is tricky: no room for clutter, no drilling, and it may need to move house next year. These ideas are compact, portable and genuinely useful. Order early – retailers often warn that delivery slows in December.

For the first-flat mover

A good-quality toolkit in a small case

Screwdrivers, an Allen key set and a tape measure cover most flat-pack and moving-day jobs.

A set of matching storage baskets

Instant order for open shelves, and they stack flat when it's time to move.

A plug-in rechargeable torch

Handy for power cuts and finding the fuse box in a flat you don't know yet.

For the one who's always cold

A heated throw

Warms one person rather than the whole flat. Check the auto shut-off and the UK plug before you buy.

A draught excluder for the front door

Rented flats are often draughty, and this needs no fixings.

Thermal-lined curtains

Pair with a tension rod or the existing rail. Measure the window first.

For the plant lover with no garden

A self-watering windowsill planter

Herbs on the sill, with fewer forgotten waterings.

A plant stand

Gets plants up to the light and off the carpet.

For the cook in a tiny kitchen

Nesting mixing bowls or measuring cups

A whole set in the space of one bowl.

An in-drawer knife organiser

Safe knife storage without a magnetic wall strip, which usually needs drilling.

A personalised chopping board

A keepsake that's also used every day.

For the kitchen-table home worker

A laptop stand that folds flat

Better posture, then it disappears into a drawer at dinner time.

A desk lamp with a warm light setting

Makes a dining table feel like a workspace, and back again.

Stocking fillers

  • Removable adhesive hooks – a renter's best friend
  • Cable tidies for the TV corner
  • A smart plug for lamps without a ceiling light
  • Scented candles or reed diffusers
