Magnolia walls and a landlord's sofa don't have to be the end of the story. These changes all go with you when you move.

Light

Layer lamps instead of relying on the ceiling light

A floor lamp in one corner and a table lamp in another makes a small room feel wider and cosier. Warm-white bulbs suit evenings.

{{amazon:floor lamp|Shop on Amazon UK}}

Lean a large mirror

A floor-standing mirror opposite or beside a window bounces daylight round the room, with no fixings.

{{amazon:full length floor mirror|Shop on Amazon UK}}

Colour and texture

Cover the sofa

A fitted stretch cover or a large throw changes the room's colour in minutes. Measure the sofa's width and arm height first.

{{amazon:stretch sofa cover|Shop on Amazon UK}}

Put a rug over the carpet

A rug defines the seating area and hides a tired carpet. Use a non-slip underlay so it stays put.

{{amazon:rug for carpet non slip|Shop on Amazon UK}}

Peel-off options – with care

Removable wallpaper and decals exist, but how cleanly they come off depends on the paint underneath. Ask your landlord, and test a small patch.

Layout

Pull furniture off the walls

Even a small gap behind the sofa can make a room feel more considered than pushing everything to the edges.

Choose furniture with legs

Seeing floor underneath a sofa or sideboard makes a room read as bigger.

Use a bookcase as a room divider

In a studio or open-plan flat, an open cube unit separates living and sleeping space while letting light through.

{{amazon:open cube bookcase room divider|Shop on Amazon UK}}

Bigger buys

A large machine-washable rug

A living-room-sized rug covers a tired carpet and pulls the seating area together, and a washable one copes with spills, pets and the end-of-tenancy clean. Measure so at least the front legs of the sofa sit on it. Roughly £30–£170 depending on size, at the time of writing – check current price.

{{amazon:large washable rug living room|Shop on Amazon UK}}

A nest of tables

Three surfaces when friends are round, one small footprint the rest of the time – better than a fixed coffee table in a narrow room. Typically around £50–£120 at the time of writing – check current price.

{{amazon:nest of tables set of 3|Shop on Amazon UK}}

A storage footstool

Extra seating, a footrest and a hiding place for throws and remotes in one. Pop a tray on top and it doubles as a coffee table. Usually under £100 at the time of writing – check current price.

{{amazon:storage footstool|Shop on Amazon UK}}

A fold-out chair bed or single sofa bed

Gives a small flat a guest bed without giving up a room. Check the folded and unfolded lengths against your floor space. Typically around £150–£250 at the time of writing – check current price.

{{amazon:fold out chair bed single|Shop on Amazon UK}}

The finishing touches

  • Cushions in two or three colours that repeat round the room
  • Plants or good artificial ones on a stand
  • Art on a picture ledge or simply leaned on a sideboard
  • A storage ottoman that doubles as a coffee table
