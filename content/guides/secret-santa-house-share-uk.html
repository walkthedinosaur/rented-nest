Secret Santa is the easiest way to do presents in a house share: one gift each, a fixed budget, and nobody spends December worrying about who to buy for. Here's how to run it – plus gifts that get a laugh and then actually get used, from a colour-changing bulb to a speaker that sticks to the shower tiles.

How to run it

  1. Agree a budget. £10–£15 is common in house shares (stuck at £10? see Secret Santa ideas under £10). Agree it in the group chat so nobody overspends.
  2. Draw names fairly. Use a free online name-drawing tool so nobody sees the list.
  3. Add a wishlist line. Ask everyone for one thing they'd like and one thing they wouldn't.
  4. Pick a date. Before the first person goes home for Christmas.
