## Key figures (from the GOV.UK chapters, read 21 Oct 2026 – WRITER MUST re-check each number against the source page
## and annex tables 1.9–1.11 before publishing; quote the exact wording, keep the year)
Source: English Housing Survey 2024 to 2025: headline findings on housing quality and energy efficiency (MHCLG,
published 29 January 2026). Interviews Mar 2024–Mar 2025; physical surveys Mar 2023–Mar 2025, weighted to April 2024.
- All homes: 5% (1.4 million) had a damp problem in 2024, up on 2022.
- Private rented: **10%** had a damp problem in 2024 (social rented 7%, owner occupied 4%). PRS 9% in 2022 → 10% in
  2024 was NOT a statistically significant rise – say so; don't write "damp is rising in rentals".
- By type, private rented: penetrating damp 4%, rising damp 3%, serious condensation/mould 6% (owner occupied 2%,
  social rented 5%). Multiple types of damp: PRS 2.9% v social 1.2% v owner occupied 0.7%.
- Self-reported (interview): 46% of private renting households said their home had condensation, damp or mould
  (owner occupiers 22%, social renters 41%). The report notes self-reported levels are "substantially higher" than
  surveyor-recorded damp – explain the difference honestly (everyday window condensation v a surveyed damp problem).
- Inadequate ventilation affected about 1% of homes, and 60% of those had damp – strongest link in the report.
  (Ties to our condensation guide's ventilation section.)
- Decent Homes: private rented had the highest share of non-decent homes, 22% (about 1.1 million), down from 29% in 2014.
- Category 1 hazards: private rented 10% in 2024 (12% in 2022); 3% of PRS homes (125,000) had more than one.
  Excess cold Category 1 hazard: 3% of private rented homes.
- Energy efficiency (chapter 2): PRS average SAP 66 in 2024 (64 in 2019); band C 48%, band D 37%, band E 9%.
  Do not state a band A–C or F–G share unless the source gives it.
- Optional context, clearly labelled as a poll not official statistics: Citizens Advice / YouGov (2,015 private
  renters in England, 12–14 June 2024): 45% experiencing damp, mould or excessive cold; 37% of those had never complained,
  51% of them citing fear of retaliation. Date it ("in a 2024 poll") – it predates the Renters' Rights Act.
