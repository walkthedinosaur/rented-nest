## Facts (Shelter England "Landlord entering without notice or permission", last updated 5 May 2026, read 4 Nov 2026)
- For inspections the landlord must give "at least 24 hours' notice in writing" and come at a reasonable time of day.
- Gas/electrical safety checks: they should confirm the visit by email or letter.
- You don't have to agree to inspections or viewings unless your agreement says you should.
- Repairs you've reported in writing: let the landlord/contractors in. Refusing can cause problems (breach, references,
  deposit).
- Entering without notice or permission "could be harassment" – even if they have a key. In a shared house the landlord
  can't enter your bedroom unless you agree.
- Practical: ask for ID, suggest another time, have someone with you; Shelter has separate harassment guidance.
- Writer must check and source separately (the page doesn't cover them): emergency access (e.g. gas leak, flood);
  changing the locks; council private-housing/tenancy-relations team as the next step. Any Renters' Rights Act change to
  access rules – check GOV.UK tenant guide; if nothing found, don't claim one.
