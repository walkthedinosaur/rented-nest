## Facts (Stroud District Council "Using electric storage heaters" leaflet, read 4 Nov 2026 – re-check at writing)
- Input = how much heat is stored overnight. Manual: high in winter, medium in autumn/spring; follow the seasons rather
  than resetting daily. Auto models: find a comfortable setting and leave it.
- Output = how fast heat is released. "Most economical" at minimum overnight; turn up only when you need warmth; down/off
  when out. If you run out of heat by evening, raise the input rather than adding plug-in heaters (cost more to run).
- Boost uses peak-rate electricity – only when stored heat has run out.
- Storage heaters work best on Economy 7; cheap hours "typically" midnight–7am winter / 1am–8am summer – check with your
  supplier. Economy 10 = 7 night + 3 day cheap hours. Check the meter's Night/Low/Rate 2 reading.
- Immersion: heat the cylinder overnight, keep the timer clock right.
- Writer must verify before using: prepayment meters can be Economy 7 (two-rate) – confirm with Ofgem/Citizens Advice
  wording; Warm Home Discount 2026/27 eligibility (GOV.UK); EPC minimum E for private lets (GOV.UK). No £/day or unit
  prices unless from a dated official source – otherwise "not verified".
