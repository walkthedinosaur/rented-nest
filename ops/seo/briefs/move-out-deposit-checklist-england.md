## Facts (Shelter, read 28 Oct 2026 – re-check at writing)
"Can landlords charge for cleaning and gardening?" (last updated 6 May 2024 – check for a newer version):
- Clean to the same standard as when you moved in (as recorded on the inventory/check-in report).
- A clause requiring cleaning "to a professional standard" is only fair if it was professionally cleaned at move-in; a clause
  making you pay for professional cleaning is unfair and can't be enforced (fees banned).
- Remove rubbish that won't fit in the bins. Garden: maintain, return in similar state; no need to improve it.
- Can't deduct just because redecoration is due, or for fair wear and tear. Deductions possible for significant changes
  without consent (e.g. cat flap, painting walls a different colour, changing locks/flooring) or damage needing redecoration.
- Carpets: the page doesn't cover carpet wear specifically – the writer must find a source (Shelter damage page, the
  deposit schemes' guidance) or leave it general ("fair wear and tear vs damage").
"How to get your deposit back" (last updated 1 May 2026):
- Ask in writing before you leave; end the tenancy properly; check the deposit is protected (DPS, mydeposits or TDS);
  all three schemes have a free dispute service; find your inventory/check-in report.
- Refund timescale: on a separate Shelter section ("How long for a deposit refund?") – read it and quote exactly. The
  GOV.UK deposit-protection page couldn't be fetched this run; re-try at writing.
Shelter "If your pet causes damage" (1 May 2026): pet damage beyond fair wear and tear can be deducted.
