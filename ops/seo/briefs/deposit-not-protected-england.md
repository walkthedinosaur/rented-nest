## Facts gathered (re-verify at writing)
Shelter England, "If your landlord does not protect your deposit" (last updated 1 May 2026, read 11 Nov 2026):
- For assured and assured shorthold tenancies the landlord must protect the deposit in a scheme within 30 days of payment
  and give you written information about it. Lodgers' deposits don't need protecting.
- If not protected / information not given: you may be able to claim the deposit back plus compensation of up to three
  times the deposit (court claim may be needed); a written settlement offer is possible; you can't be evicted on some
  grounds while it's unprotected (section 21 abolished from 1 May 2026; for section 8, the court can't order eviction in
  some cases, but other grounds can still apply).
GOV.UK "Tenancy deposit protection" (read 11 Nov 2026): the three schemes are Deposit Protection Service, MyDeposits,
Tenancy Deposit Scheme.
