## Facts to verify on the day (open each page; don't copy these blind)
- GOV.UK Renters' Rights Act overview for tenants (https://www.gov.uk/guidance/renters-rights-act-overview-for-tenants,
  read 19 Nov): a landlord can evict to sell or move in, but "not ... within the first 12 months of the tenancy"; notice
  is usually four months. Confirm the ground number (1A for selling) and any re-letting restriction on the GOV.UK
  grounds-for-possession tenant guidance before stating them.
- Shelter "Landlord selling your home?" (https://england.shelter.org.uk/housing_advice/private_renting/landlord_selling_renters_rights,
  updated 1 May 2026): you don't have to leave straight away; the landlord needs a section 8 notice, then a court order,
  then bailiffs; if sold with you in it the new owner becomes your landlord; check you have a new home before accepting
  money to leave; don't move out too early or the council may treat you as intentionally homeless.
- Shelter "If your home is sold to a new landlord" (…/landlord_selling_renters_rights/after_sale) – deposit and
  tenancy carry on; read before writing that section.
- Viewings: reuse the 24-hours-written-notice wording from our landlord-entry guide (Shelter-sourced).
