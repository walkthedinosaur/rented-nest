## Intent
A first-time renter (student, graduate, first solo flat) making a shopping and to-do list before or just after moving in.

## Angle
Two-part checklist: (A) day-one checks that protect your deposit and safety, (B) what to buy, by room, split into
"day one", "first month" and "can wait". Renter-specific: check the inventory before buying, nothing that needs
drilling, small-space versions. Printable feel (checkbox lists), but no new PDF/lead magnet – the deposit checklist
(register #11) is the lead magnet; link to it once live.

## Outline
1. Short answer + "Ad" disclosure (automatic).
2. Day-one checks (no products): read the inventory and photograph every room with dates; meter readings; find the
   stopcock and fuse box; test smoke alarm (and CO alarm where there's a fuel-burning appliance) – verify current
   landlord duties on GOV.UK; check you've got the deposit protection details, gas safety record, EPC and the
   Renters' Rights Act information sheet (verify which documents apply for new tenancies from 1 May 2026 on GOV.UK).
   Link Renters' Rights Act guide.
3. Kitchen essentials (day one / first month / can wait).
4. Bathroom and cleaning – incl. squeegee and microfibre cloths for condensation (link condensation guide).
5. Bedroom – bedding, blackout or thermal curtains on a tension rod if no rail (link draught-proofing / stay-warm).
6. Living room on a budget (link small living room ideas, magnolia walls).
7. Storage without drilling (link no-drill storage, Command strips).
8. Tools and safety – compact toolkit, extension lead with surge protection, torch, first aid kit.
9. What not to buy yet – furniture before measuring, anything already on the inventory, wall-mounted items.
10. FAQ (3–4) from the secondary queries.
11. Sources.
