## Facts to verify on the day
- Notice: at least 24 hours in writing, reasonable time (Shelter – same source as our landlord-entry guide; re-open it).
- Whether any law sets a maximum number of inspections: state only what Shelter/GOV.UK/Citizens Advice say. If none
  states a number, say "no fixed legal limit" only if a source says so; otherwise say the sources don't set one and the
  test is "reasonable" + your right to live without unnecessary disturbance (quiet enjoyment – find the Shelter wording).
- Do NOT present "every 3–6 months is common" as fact unless a GOV.UK/Shelter/CA page says it; the MSE replies are
  opinions – can be described as "on forums, most landlords said…" without numbers if unsure.
