## Diagnosis
Reach, not content, is the bottleneck. We have 32 guides but almost nobody sees them: Pinterest is still holding a
new account back, Google takes months, Instagram is tiny. The money window is the next 6 weeks (Black Friday 27 Nov,
Christmas). Every channel should point at the gift pages that already convert, and we need one channel that doesn't
depend on Pinterest's trust score.
