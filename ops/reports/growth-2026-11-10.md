## C6b. Pinterest Trends (UK) – working again
**Root cause of the blank pages on 22 Oct and 2 Nov:** the playbook URL used `country=GB`; Trends' code for the UK is
**`GB+IE`** (Great Britain and Ireland), and detail pages also need `&dateRange=…&aggregationLevel=2`. Even then, most
of our fixed keywords say "Keyword unavailable for this region" (dehumidifier, christmas gift ideas tested). What works
is the **search page filtered by word**: `trends.pinterest.com/search/?country=GB%2BIE&keywordsToInclude=<word>` –
it lists growing keywords containing that word with weekly/monthly/yearly change. Playbook 02 step 6b and
