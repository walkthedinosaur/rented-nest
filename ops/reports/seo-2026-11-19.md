## FAQ additions (registers #80, #83) – facts from pages opened today, linked in the text
- **can-landlord-put-rent-up-england**: "My landlord asked me to suggest a fair rent – what should I offer?" Covers
  comparable lets and the repairs you've done. Shelter Legal (updated 24 Aug 2026): an agreed increase after 1 May 2026
  isn't valid unless it follows a valid section 13 notice, and it can't exceed the notice figure. GOV.UK: open market rent.
- **move-out-deposit-checklist-england**: "What about an old deposit I never collected?" GOV.UK lists the 3 schemes. Shelter
  (updated 9 May 2024): custodial schemes pay out after 2 weeks with no contact or no reply, using a statutory declaration
  witnessed by a solicitor (can cost £5). For insurance schemes, tell the scheme if there's no reply or refund within 10
  days. No claim time limit is stated, because no source gives one.
- **give-notice-to-landlord-england**: "I'm buying a house – when should I give notice?" Shelter: only give notice if you
  can move out when it ends, and it must end on the first/last day of a rent period. GOV.UK How to buy: the completion date
  is confirmed at exchange, often around 2–4 weeks later. GOV.UK: a shorter notice period or early leaving needs the
  landlord's agreement in writing. Second FAQ: "We're students in a shared house – can one of us give notice?" Shelter
  student tenancies page (updated 19 Oct 2026): rolling tenancies, usually 2 months, one joint tenant's valid notice ends
  it for everyone, the landlord can agree shorter notice in writing, halls differ.
- All three guides have `updated: 2026-11-19` and new source lines. These are general information, not legal advice.
